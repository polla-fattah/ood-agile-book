*Designing, Testing and Evolving Software with AI Assistance*

{{< book-covers >}}

{{< book-toc depth=2 >}}

This textbook guides postgraduate and advanced students from writing classes to designing, testing, reviewing, and changing maintainable software in a collaborative team workflow. Its central teaching unit is a software change: understand requested behavior, choose an object-oriented design, protect it with automated tests, inspect results, and use feedback to plan subsequent iterations.

### Core Structure

- **Part I: Foundations & Object-Oriented Principles (Chapters 1–6)**
  - Chapter 1: Developing Software That Can Change
  - Chapter 2: Object-Oriented Development Beyond Syntax
  - Chapter 3: Agile Development and the Nature of Change
  - Chapter 4: From Requirements to an Iteration
  - Chapter 5: Responsibilities, Extension, and Substitutability (SOLID)
  - Chapter 6: Interfaces, Dependencies, and Composition
- **Part II: Testing, Creation & Behavioral Patterns (Chapters 7–9)**
  - Chapter 7: Testing as a Design Practice (TDD & Test Doubles)
  - Chapter 8: Creating and Connecting Objects (Factory, Builder, Dependency Injection)
  - Chapter 9: Designing Changing Behaviour (Strategy, State, Observer, Command)
- **Part III: Architecture, Packages & Legacy Systems (Chapters 10–11)**
  - Chapter 10: From Classes to Application Structure (Layered Architecture & Package Principles)
  - Chapter 11: Refactoring and Legacy Code (Characterisation Tests, Seams, Technical Debt)
- **Part IV: Metrics, Delivery, Evolution & AI Assistance (Chapters 12–15)**
  - Chapter 12: Measuring Progress, Flow and Technical Health (Lead Time, Cycle Time, Throughput, Health)
  - Chapter 13: Integration, Delivery and Feedback (Milestone 4, Contract Tests, Feature Flags)
  - Chapter 14: Responding to Change and Handing Over
  - Chapter 15: AI as a Software Engineering Assistant
- **Appendices A–F**
  - Appendix A: Programming Readiness and Notation (UML & Diagnostics)
  - Appendix B: Tools and Reproducible Setup
  - Appendix C: Engineering Templates
  - Appendix D: Checkpoints and Glossary
  - Appendix E: Critical Reading and Project Evidence
  - Appendix F: Software Engineering for AI and Data Components
