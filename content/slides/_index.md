Lecture decks supporting the weekly schedule of *Object Oriented Development and Agile Practices*. 

Each slide deck correlates with the weekly reading and practical activities:
- **Presentation Mode**: Open any deck, use arrow keys or the space bar to navigate, and press **F** for fullscreen.
- **Handouts & Export**: Decks format to one slide per page in landscape orientation for printable handouts or PDF generation (`Ctrl + P`).
