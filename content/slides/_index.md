## Using a deck

1. **Navigate:** the arrow keys or the space bar move between slides.
2. **Present:** press {{< kbd "F" >}} for fullscreen.
3. **Print or export:** decks format to one slide per page in landscape orientation, so {{< kbd "Ctrl" "P" >}} gives a handout or a PDF.
